Privacy policy

The "rclone NAS backup" app is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

It uses Google Drive access only to store and read encrypted backup files from the owner's home NAS, in the owner's own Google Drive.

What it does not do

It does not collect data from other people. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use Google user data for advertising or for training AI models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
